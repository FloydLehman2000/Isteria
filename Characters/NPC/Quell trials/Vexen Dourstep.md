### 🧍 **General Profile**
- **Age:** Adult elf, gray inn skintone.
- **Ancestry:** **Elf**
- **Appearance:** Slim figure with quick and dark clothing on keeping him mostly in the dark. He also wears his iconic hat with a wide brim and a single feather.
- **Aura:** Smart with his words and carefull not to meet things up. Stealthy and quick, good with daggers and electronics.
- **Mannerisms:** Quick, careful and loyal to the dollmaker.

---
### 📖 **Origins**
- A loyal ex-knight of gepetto.
- Served the Dollmaker as one of the first, but got exiled because of his dealings with shade people like Thorne Quell.
- Arriving on the desolate chain to find the Dollmaker, his mission is to find and kill the Dollmaker.
- He was one of the first knights, and got exiled long before Sir Clank entered.

---
### Mission
* Assasinate the dollmaker and get revenge on the order of the [[Knights of Gepetto]] for exiling them.
