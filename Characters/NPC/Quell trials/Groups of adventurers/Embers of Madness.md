## Embers of Madness

## Backstory

The Embers of Madness were never meant to be a team. They are four walking disasters, each with a temper too short, a thirst for battle too great, and an almost complete inability to accept being told what to do. They don't believe in careful planning, diplomacy, or retreat. To them, the world is a place full of things that need hitting, and the only question worth asking is who gets to hit them first. Their unlikely partnership began in a fighting pit somewhere along the outskirts of the Desolate Chain. Each had entered for their own reasons, but all four shared the same appetite for violence. When a brawl broke out that engulfed the entire pit, the four found themselves fighting side by side, not out of loyalty, but because the remaining opponents were getting in the way of their own fights. By the time the dust settled, the pit was in ruins, the crowd was fleeing, and the four were the only ones still standing. Since then, they've travelled together, leaving broken doors, battered mercenaries, and demolished taverns in their wake. Their greatest strength is also their greatest weakness: none of them knows when to stop. They argue over everything, from the direction of travel to who gets the last drink. Whenever a disagreement becomes too heated, they settle it the only way they know how — by fighting until someone gives in. Somehow, this has become their decision-making process, and somehow, it works. Beneath the chaos lies a strange form of trust. They know exactly how far they can push one another, and each understands that the others will never abandon a good fight. Their methods are brutal, but they have survived encounters that have killed far more disciplined groups. They don't outthink their enemies. They overwhelm them.

The Quell Trials caught their attention for one simple reason: Krallax is the biggest fight any of them has ever heard of. They couldn't care less about the politics, the promises of glory, or the truth behind the Trials. They want to face the mechanical dragon and discover whether it can withstand everything they have to offer. Yet even these battle-hungry fools are beginning to realize that something is wrong. Too many adventurers have vanished, too many stories end in failure, and the people running the Trials seem far too comfortable sending others to their deaths. The Embers of Madness aren't interested in ending a cycle of death and regret out of compassion. They want to break the cycle because they refuse to accept that there is a fight they cannot win. And if Krallax stands in their way, then Krallax can learn the same lesson as everyone else. Never stand between the Embers and a fight.

## Adventurers

### 🔥 1. Rage Crest — The Unbound Fury

Class: Warrior
Role: Primary damage dealer, relentless aggressor
Personality: Explosive, fearless, fiercely competitive, incapable of backing down
Traits: Rage Crest fights as though every battle is personal. She wears a collection of broken weapon fragments around her neck, each taken from an opponent who managed to land a memorable blow. She laughs when injured, grows louder as the odds turn against her, and considers retreat the greatest insult imaginable. Despite her temper, she has an instinctive sense for the flow of combat and knows exactly when an enemy is about to falter.
Rage Crest is the spark that sets the group ablaze. She charges into fights before anyone has finished explaining the situation, often turning a manageable confrontation into an all-out brawl. Her greatest weakness is her need to prove herself. If an enemy challenges her strength, she will pursue them to the ends of the earth just to settle the score.

She and Proudfist constantly compete for dominance, each convinced that the other has no idea how a proper fight should be conducted.
Dialogue options:
- "You call that a hit? Come on, put some bloody effort into it!"
- "If it can bleed, it can fall. If it can't bleed, I'll find a way."
- "Proudfist! Tell your plan to move faster. My fists are getting bored!"

### 🐕 2. The Dog — The Quiet Bite

Class: Brawler
Role: Close-quarters fighter, grappler, team anchor
Personality: Passive, patient, dry-witted, surprisingly perceptive
Traits: The Dog rarely starts an argument and almost never raises his voice. He lets the others shout, threaten, and swing at one another while he quietly finishes his meal. When violence finally arrives, however, he becomes terrifyingly persistent. He grabs, pins, and pummels opponents with the determination of a hound that has found its prey and refuses to let go.
Nobody knows The Dog's real name, and nobody has ever managed to convince him to share it. The nickname began after a particularly ugly fight in which he refused to release an opponent's arm until the man surrendered. The name stuck, and he has never cared enough to correct anyone.
Of the four, he is the closest thing the Embers of Madness have to a voice of reason. He rarely offers advice, but when he does speak, the others tend to listen. He has a habit of watching people rather than talking to them, and he often recognizes danger long before his companions do.
The Dog doesn't fight for glory. He fights because he enjoys the simplicity of it. There are no complicated promises in a fight, no political games, and no hidden intentions. Someone stands against you, and you find out who is stronger.

Dialogue options:
- "You done shouting? Good. I was getting tired."
- "Proudfist says we go left. Rage says we go right. I'll go wherever the punching starts."
- "That's a big dragon. Reckon it'll let me get close?"

### 👊 3. Proudfist — The King of Broken Teeth

Class: Guardian
Role: Frontline leader, defender, tactical bruiser
Personality: Proud, commanding, stubborn, fiercely protective of his reputation
Traits: Proudfist believes that strength earns respect and that respect earns the right to lead. His body is covered in old scars, and his oversized gauntlets have been repaired so many times that they resemble scrap metal held together by sheer spite. He demands obedience, but he never asks his companions to endure something he wouldn't face himself.
Proudfist is the closest thing the Embers of Madness have to a leader. He makes the calls, sets their direction, and decides which fights are worth pursuing. The others follow him not because they respect authority, but because he has beaten every one of them at least once.
His leadership style is unconventional. If Rage Crest refuses an order, he challenges her. If Battlehide disagrees with a plan, they settle it with a contest of strength. If The Dog simply walks away, Proudfist usually sighs and follows him, knowing that the quiet brawler has probably spotted something the others missed.
Despite his arrogance, Proudfist has an unshakable loyalty to his companions. He will take a blow meant for any of them, though he will complain about it for the next three days. He considers his team a reflection of his own strength, and he would rather die than let anyone claim that the Embers of Madness abandoned one of their own.

Dialogue options:
- "I don't need you to like me. I need you to follow me until the enemy is dead."
- "We fight together. We argue together. And if one of us falls, the rest make damn sure something falls with them."
- "Krallax, is it? Good. I've been looking for something worthy of breaking these hands on."

### 🛡️ 4. Battlehide — The Walking Fortress

Class: Seraph
Role: Divine frontline, battlefield support, unbreakable vanguard
Personality: Boisterous, confrontational, fiercely loyal, enjoys testing limits
Traits: Battlehide is a towering warrior whose battered armor bears the marks of countless battles. She believes pain is a teacher, fear is a challenge, and a fight is wasted if nobody learns something from it. Her divine power manifests in flashes of scorching light that reinforce her defenses and empower her allies. She is just as likely to headbutt an enemy as she is to offer them a chance to surrender.
Battlehide earned her name by surviving a battle in which an entire mercenary company attempted to break through her position. She held the line long enough for her former companions to escape, emerging from the wreckage with shattered armor and a grin that frightened the survivors more than the fighting itself.
Her connection to divine power is a mystery even to her. She speaks of her blessings as though they were another set of muscles, something to be strengthened through use. She has little patience for religious ceremony or holy doctrine. If a higher power wants her to do something, it can come down and tell her to her face.
Battlehide is the group's most enthusiastic supporter of Proudfist's leadership, though she challenges his decisions almost as often as Rage Crest does. She believes a leader who never has to defend his position will eventually become weak.
Of all four, she is the most excited about facing Krallax. Not because she thinks the dragon will be easy to defeat, but because she wants to discover whether even her divine gifts have a limit.

Dialogue options:
- "Come on, hit harder! I've had priests bless me with more pain than that!"
- "If the gods wanted me to be sensible, they shouldn't have made me this difficult to kill."
- "Proudfist, I trust you with my life. Your decisions, however, remain questionable."

## Running the Embers of Madness at the Table

The Embers should feel like a powder keg waiting for a spark. They interrupt each other, turn minor disagreements into contests, and treat almost every obstacle as an invitation to fight. Yet their chaotic behavior hides a genuinely effective combat unit. Proudfist protects the others and calls targets, Rage Crest relentlessly pressures the biggest threat, The Dog grapples and disables enemies, and Battlehide holds the frontline while bolstering her companions with divine power. When the party encounters them, the Embers may already be arguing about how to approach Krallax. Rage Crest wants to charge straight at him, Battlehide wants to test his strength head-on, and Proudfist insists that they need a plan. The Dog sits nearby, sharpening a blade and waiting for the shouting to stop. Their initial reaction to the party: They are suspicious of anyone who talks about ending the Quell Trials without being willing to fight for it. They respect strength, determination, and honesty far more than titles or grand speeches.

What could convince them to join forces: A chance to fight Krallax alongside people who are willing to stand their ground. If the party explains the cycle of death and regret, Proudfist may take the idea seriously, but the others will need convincing through action rather than words.
Their greatest weakness: They cannot easily agree on a strategy when their pride is on the line. An enemy capable of provoking them into fighting separately could dismantle the team. Their greatest strength, however, is that none of them will willingly leave another Ember behind.
The Embers of Madness don't need to like each other. They just need something big enough to hit together.
