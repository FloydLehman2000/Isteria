## 🧍 General Profile
---
- **Age:** mid–40s (Infernis resilience makes him appear closer to late 30s).
- **Ancestry:** **Infernis** — violet skin with a faint ember-like glow beneath old scars; horns once elegant, now jagged and broken at the tips.
- **Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, favoring long military-style coats reinforced with stolen mechanical plating. One horn bears a permanent break — the fragment Atlas keeps in her locket. His left arm is rumored to be grafted with clanker tech, concealed beneath a black steel gauntlet.
- **Aura:** Charismatic but suffocating, like smoke slowly filling a sealed room.
- **Mannerisms:** Slow, deliberate speech. Rarely raises his voice. When he smiles, it is predatory — measured, intentional.

---
## 📖 Origins
---
Crane was born in **Ashvane**, a forgotten fishing village on one of the southern isles of the Desolate Chain. As an Infernis, he was marked from birth — feared, blamed, and quietly despised. Every misfortune found a way to land at his feet.

He learned early that trust was a liability. Survival meant acting first, striking harder, and never allowing yourself to be cornered.  
_If you control the knife, you never feel it in your back._

As a teenager, Crane fled Ashvane by stowing away on a merchant vessel. From there, he carved a living among smugglers, dock gangs, and trial-runaways — learning trade routes, supply lines, and the value of scarcity long before he ever wielded power.

---
## 🪶 The Atlas Years
---
Before Ironvein, Crane led a small but ruthless band of outlaws operating between the early settlements of the Chain. It was during this time that he took in **Erlenne Driftwing**.

He gave her the name **Atlas** — a shield, a burden, and a reminder of ownership. Their bond was forged through survival and mutual exile, but twisted by Crane’s need for control. He taught her how to steal, fight, disappear, and endure — never how to leave.

When Atlas turned on their comrades, breaking the chain he had wrapped around her, Crane vanished without a word. All that remained was the shattered horn she carried with her — and the certainty that betrayal had cost him something he considered his.

---
## 🔥 Rise of the Ironvein Syndicate
---
After the first two years of the **Quell Trials**, hope of defeating Krallax had all but vanished. Settlements hardened into cities, power struggles erupted, and the Desolate Chain entered an era of permanence.

On **Varnhal**, **High Regent Cealith Veyron** consolidated rule from **Highthrone Bastion**, forging a monarchy where chaos once reigned. Yet while the crown claimed authority over people, **trade remained lawless**.

Crane saw the opening.

Rather than oppose Highthrone’s rise, he aligned with it — offering structure where none existed. Through coercion, negotiation, and quiet violence, he merged gangs, absorbed fleets, and crushed rivals. Where others sought thrones, Crane sought **supply**.

Thus, the **Ironvein Syndicate** was born — not as a rebellion, but as a necessary evil. By monopolizing trade, shipping, and material flow across the Chain, Ironvein became indispensable. Highthrone gained stability. Crane gained immunity.

---
## ⚔️ Current Role in Ironfell
---
Crane personally oversees **Ironfell**, the Syndicate’s shadow heart.

Officially abandoned, the arena now serves Ironvein as:
- A **recruitment ground**, where survivors earn a place in the Syndicate’s fleets.
- A **testing pit**, where prisoners, unruly assets, and captured adventurers are broken or refined.
- A **spectacle for elites**, where High Regent loyalists and wealthy patrons wager in secrecy — their debts binding them to Crane’s will.

Ironfell is not indulgence. It is doctrine.

For Crane, the arena embodies his belief:  
**freedom is a lie; survival exists only within chains.**

And personally, Ironfell is where he intends to confront Atlas — not to kill her, but to prove that rebellion leads only back to him.

---
## 🎯 Goals
---
1. **Monopolize trade across the Desolate Chain** through Ironvein fleets.
2. **Secure the Dollmaker**, granting Ironvein mechanical supremacy.
3. **Subjugate the Dawn Mercy**, reshaping the cult into an engine of obedience.
4. **Break Atlas**, not in body but in belief — proving her escape was meaningless.

---
## 🕸️ Methods
---
- **Fear & Obsession:** Loyalty is enforced through fear, maintained through paranoia.
- **Divide & Conquer:** Lieutenants are rotated and rivaled to prevent unity.
- **Symbols:** The **Ironvein chain insignia** is burned into docks, hulls, and captives alike.
- **Calculated Cruelty:** Betrayal is punished publicly and brutally; success is rewarded — but never enough to allow freedom.

# 💥 Crane’s True Weakness

Years ago, Crane experimented with **arcane core compression** — attempting to stabilize volatile energy for transport off-island.
It failed.
The core detonated — partially.
Instead of killing him, the energy fused into him.

Now:
- Arcane energy builds within his body.
- If not stabilized weekly, it destabilizes violently.
- If it reaches critical mass… he doesn’t die quietly.

He becomes a **human arcane bomb**.
