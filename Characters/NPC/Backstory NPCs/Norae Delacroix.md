### 🧍 **General Profile**

- **Age:** Elderly by human standards; biologically stabilized beyond natural aging through Enclave preservation rites. Estimated 90–120 years, though inconsistently recorded due to memory-binding effects.
- **Ancestry:** Human
- **Appearance:** Slender and composed, with carefully kept robes layered in muted lunar tones. His skin carries faint, silver-threaded scars—marks of repeated memory-binding rituals used to anchor divine archives into the body. His eyes appear calm, but never fully rest on a single point for long, as though constantly cross-referencing unseen texts.
- **Aura:** Quietly authoritative. His presence does not demand attention—it reorganizes it. Conversations near him tend to become structured, deliberate, and unusually careful.
- **Mannerisms:** Speaks in measured cadence, often repeating phrases in slightly altered forms as if verifying meaning. Frequently pauses mid-sentence when recalling “sealed” or restricted knowledge. Touches written material as though it might respond.
---
### 📖 **Origins**
- Norae Delacroix began as a **librarian-theologian** within the early Luma Enclave, specializing in fragmented divine records and pre-Ascension celestial cartography.
- Early in his career, he gained recognition for reconstructing incomplete myth cycles of **Lady Luma**, arguing that divine memory was not metaphor, but structure.
- He pioneered the doctrine of **“Continuity Through Confirmation”**:
    - If a divine record is incomplete, it must be completed through ritual reenactment.
    - If a god cannot be observed directly, it must be reconstructed through relic convergence.
- This philosophy elevated him rapidly within the Enclave hierarchy, as it offered what many scholars secretly desired: **proof instead of faith**.
---
### 📚 **The Luma Enclave**
- A secluded scholarly order dedicated to preserving and validating divine memory across Isteria.
- Unlike traditional worship groups, the Enclave treats divinity as an **archival problem rather than a theological one**.
- Their primary goal is not worship of Lady Luma, but **verification of her continued existence through artifacts, relics, and controlled ritual convergence**.
- Members are trained to treat belief as secondary to reproducible phenomenon.

Norae became one of its central architects.

---
### 🕯️ **Doctrine of Reassembly**
- Norae developed the guiding principle behind the Enclave’s modern operations:
    - Divine fragments scattered across Isteria are not symbolic—they are functional.
    - When properly aligned, they restore “truth states” of forgotten gods.
- This doctrine justified increasingly dangerous expeditions into sealed ruins, collapsed sanctums, and unstable relic sites such as **Wyrmbane’s Hollow**.
- Critics within the Enclave warned that reconstruction risked **interpretation replacing origin**.
- Norae dismissed this concern as “fear of incomplete understanding.”

---
### 🔗 **The Pyxis Directive**
- Norae personally assigned Pyxis to the retrieval of **The Lantern of Stars**.
- Official reasoning: restoration of Ithar, Daughter of Luma as stabilizing divine memory.
- Unofficial motivation: confirmation of Enclave theory that **dual relic convergence produces authentic divine reconstitution**.
- Norae is aware—at least partially—that prior convergence attempts have produced **anomalous intelligences rather than confirmed deities**.
- He does not interpret this as failure.
- He interprets it as **misclassification**.

---
### 🔥 **The Hidden Conviction**
- Norae does not simply believe the gods existed.
- He believes they **must continue to exist for reality to remain coherent**.
- To him, divine absence is not freedom—it is degradation of structure.
- If a god cannot be proven to persist, then memory itself becomes unreliable.
- This belief drives him to accept escalating risk thresholds in relic convergence experiments.
He would rather awaken something uncertain than preserve something unknown.

---
### 🕸️ **Methods**
- **Ritualized Instruction:** Embeds commands within academic language, ensuring obedience feels like scholarship.
- **Relic Framing:** Reclassifies dangerous artifacts as “continuity instruments” or “memory anchors.”
- **Selective Disclosure:** Withholds full consequences of convergence from field agents like Pyxis.
- **Emotional Calibration:** Trains initiates to interpret doubt as “archival corruption,” not intuition.

---
### ⚖️ **Philosophy**
> _“A forgotten god is not gone. It is incomplete.”_

Norae Delacroix does not seek worship.  
He seeks **verification**.
To him, truth is not something discovered—it is something reconstructed.  
And if reconstruction requires distortion, then distortion becomes acceptable.
Because in Norae’s doctrine, the greatest threat is not false gods—
It is the possibility that divinity was never stable to begin with.
