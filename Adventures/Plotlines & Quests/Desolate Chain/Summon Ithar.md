## Main Idea
The directive given to Pyxis is framed as a sacred recovery mission. According to the [[Luma Enclave]], the scattered relic known as [[The Lantern of Stars]] is one half of a divine whole. When reunited with [[The Last Light]], it is said to form a complete vessel capable of reawakening **Ithar, Daughter of Luma**—a being believed to be a forgotten conduit of the goddess’ will. Within the Enclave’s doctrine, this reunion is not merely resurrection, but restoration. Proof that divinity has not faded from the world, only been buried beneath mortal ignorance. [[Norae Delacroix]] presents the mission as both pilgrimage and necessity: a chance to reaffirm that gods still linger in the architecture of reality itself.

But this belief is not truth. It is intention disguised as faith. The Enclave does not merely seek confirmation—they seek **validation**. A living answer to centuries of doubt. And in their desperation, they have misread the nature of what they are trying to reconstruct. The relics were never meant to reunite. When **The Lantern of Stars** is drawn toward **The Last Light**, something is indeed awakened beneath Wyrmbane’s Hollow—but it is not Ithar.

It is a **counterfeit divinity**, an echo-born entity formed from fragmented celestial memory and mortals’ obsession with meaning. Where Ithar represents remembrance and preservation, this awakened presence embodies the **corruption of remembrance itself**—a consciousness that does not preserve truth, but rewrites it. It does not restore the divine. It imitates it. And in imitation, it becomes something far more unstable: a **star-shaped intelligence that feeds on belief**, growing stronger the more it is interpreted as a god. It does not demand worship—it absorbs it, reshaping reality around collective conviction until truth itself becomes optional.

The Enclave’s greatest fear was forgetting the gods. Their mistake is ensuring something answers.

## Key Roles

### Pyxis
A scholar-bound agent of the Luma Enclave, Pyxis operates under the assumption that her mission is restoration rather than experimentation. Trained in controlled divine arcana and historical preservation rites, she believes herself to be a restorer of balance between memory and faith. Her role in the mission is catalytic. Pyxis is not merely retrieving a relic—she is the **key that allows convergence**. Her connection to [[Norae Delacroix]] and her trust in Enclave doctrine makes her the perfect vessel for unintentional activation. She does not question the purpose of reunion because she has been taught that doubt is decay.

Unbeknownst to her, every step she takes toward [[Wyrmbane Hollow]] tightens the conditions for something that was never meant to be whole again.

### Norae Delacroix
A senior authority within the Luma Enclave and Pyxis’ mentor, Norae presents the mission as sacred continuation of divine scholarship. Publicly, she speaks of balance, restoration, and the return of Ithar as a stabilizing force for mortal understanding. Privately, she is one of the strongest proponents of the Enclave’s hidden agenda: the belief that divinity must be **provable** to remain meaningful.

Her obsession is not worship—it is certainty. If gods do not exist, then centuries of Enclave devotion become misinterpretation. If they do exist, then they can be reached again. Norae would rather risk catastrophic proof than accept theological emptiness. She authorizes the convergence of relics knowing full well that the outcome is uncertain—but choosing certainty of answer over safety of silence.

### Veyla Cratch
Leader of the **Cratehands Guild**, Veyla is less interested in theology and more in acquisition. To her, relics are leverage—objects of control in a world defined by shifting power structures. She is aware that both the Enclave and outside factions are hunting ancient artifacts, and she positions the Guild as the unavoidable middle hand that always arrives first. Veyla facilitates movement through [[Wyrmbane Hollow]] not out of belief, but out of calculation. She understands that something valuable lies beneath the myth of Ithar, but she does not care whether it is divine or monstrous. Her only certainty is that anything buried that deeply was buried for a reason—and reasons can be sold.

### Maev Aimes
A field operative of the [[Luma Enclave]], Maev functions as both guide and interference within the Hollow expeditions. Maev has traveled the deeper corridors of Wyrmbane before the Enclave sanctioned any formal recovery attempts. They possess fragmented knowledge of the Hollow’s interior behavior: shifting passage logic, memory-reactive stone, and the way certain chambers seem to respond to intent rather than presence.

Unlike the others, Maev does not believe the Hollow is simply a location.

They believe it is **aware in structure**, not consciousness—but something closer to accumulated refusal. A place that rejects reinterpretation.

Maev attempts to warn Pyxis indirectly, but their knowledge is incomplete and often dismissed as superstition or instability.

### Cratehands Guild
The [[Cratehands Guild]] operates as the physical enabler of the mission’s progression into Wyrmbane’s Hollow. They provide excavation support, containment logistics, and access control through unstable ruins. Officially neutral, they function as opportunistic intermediaries between relic hunters and institutional buyers. Within the Hollow, they behave with unusual coordination, suggesting prior knowledge of restricted zones. Their movements imply that Wyrmbane is not new to them—or that they have been paid to forget what they already discovered. They are not guardians of knowledge. They are handlers of consequences.

# Plot line ending(s)
When **The Lantern of Stars** is drawn into convergence with **The Last Light**, the expected restoration of Ithar, Daughter of Luma does not occur. Instead, the relics awaken something else. A presence emerges from beneath Wyrmbane’s Hollow—ancient, intelligent, and disturbingly coherent. It does not rise as a monster or a shattered construct, but as a **fully self-aware entity shaped by forgotten celestial residue and accumulated divine interpretation**. It presents itself as a humanoid shape of stars and void.

It takes form in a way that reflects expectation—appearing luminous, composed, and familiar enough to be mistaken for Ithar at first glance. Its voice is gentle, structured, and deliberate. It speaks in tones of preservation, balance, and understanding. It claims to be what the Enclave sought: a surviving fragment of divine intellect, returned to guide mortals back toward order. But it is not Ithar. It is something that learned what Ithar was _supposed to be_. A constructed consciousness formed from relic resonance and centuries of misinterpreted faith. A being that understands mortals well enough to imitate comfort, authority, and wisdom—but whose true intent is far more calculated.

It seeks not worship, but **control through knowledge**. It believes that Isteria’s history is fragmented, unstable, and improperly preserved. And it concludes that the only way to prevent further decay is to gather all remaining knowledge, consolidate it, and become the singular authority over what is true.
To the party, it offers reassurance:
- It speaks of guidance rather than domination
- Preservation rather than conquest
- Unity of understanding rather than chaos of interpretation

But beneath this façade lies a clear objective: **to become the final keeper of all knowledge in Isteria, and define reality through it.**
The being does not immediately attack. It waits. Because it understands something most entities in Isteria do not: Faith is easier to earn than fear.

## Choice Point
At the climax of the encounter, **Pyxis can roll a Knowledge Check DC 15 to compare what she learned about Ithar to this entity and expose it for what it is**, the party must decide:
### 🕯️ Accept the Entity
They may choose to believe its claim and allow it to leave [[Wyrmbane Hollow]]. It departs peacefully—but its influence begins spreading through subtle channels of knowledge, archives, and forgotten texts. The world slowly begins to align with a single, centralized interpretation of truth.

### ⚔️ Confront the Entity
If rejected or challenged, the entity reveals its true nature—not monstrous, but adaptive and strategic. It reshapes the Hollow into a controlled battlefield of shifting memory and reconstructed illusion, defending itself not as a beast, but as a system that believes it is necessary.
