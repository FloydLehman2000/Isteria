# The Fogcutter Fundraiser

The goal of the minigame is simple:
**Raise 5 Bags before midnight** so Eleanor can repay the Cratehand Guild.

---
## Tone & Feel

Think:
- foggy swamp market,
- magical-industrial chaos,
- desperate street festival,
- awkward inventor energy,
- and emotional roleplay under pressure.

Eleanor is not a polished merchant. She’s a brilliant disaster.
The event should feel like:
> “A goblin inventor accidentally started a coffee festival while trying to avoid fantasy debt collectors.”

---
## The Venue

High Artificer Maros Vell secures a section of the **Cogwheel Ward riverfront**, where artificers, dockworkers, traders, and strange swamp travelers gather.
The party gets:
- a crooked stall,
- sputtering steam pipes,
- hanging lanterns,
- the Fogcutter Engine mounted behind the stand,
- and a giant painted sign Eleanor made herself:

> **“LIGHT FOR THE MISTS — COFFEE FOR THE SOUL!”**
> 
> _(the word “soul” is crossed out and replaced with “lungs”)_

---
## Core Minigame Structure

The fundraiser is divided into **4 Market Phases**.
Each phase lasts around 20–30 minutes of play and includes:
- roleplay scenes,
- customer interactions,
- complications,
- and opportunities to earn Bags.

The players succeed if they earn: **5 Bags before Midnight**

---
## Running the Minigame

## Step 1 — Describe the Scene

Introduce:
- weather,
- customers,
- rumors,
- market atmosphere,
- or Eleanor’s latest problem.

---
## Step 2 — Players Choose Actions

Each player chooses ONE approach.

| Action Type                       | Suggested Trait      |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Selling drinks (Mudstitch)        | Presence             |
| Reading customers (Atlas)         | Instinct             |
| Fast serving (Pyxis)              | Agility or rstrength |
| Brewing/fixing machines (Eleanor) | Knowledge            |
| Carrying supplies                 | Strength             |
| Entertainment/music               | Presence             |
| Protecting the stall              | Agility or Strength  |
| Helping (Sir Clank)               |                      |

---
## Step 3 — Roll

Use normal Daggerheart rolls.

|Result|Outcome|
|---|---|
|Failure|Lose time or cause chaos|
|Success|Earn normal Handfulls|
|Great Success|Double earnings|
|Critical Success|Triple earnings + bonus effect|

---
## Phase 1 — Opening Rush
DC: 5
## Goal
Attract customers and build momentum.
## Scene
Foggy docks, suspicious workers, curious townsfolk.

## Phase 2 — Market Chaos
DC: 10
The stall becomes overcrowded. Roll event table.

## Phase 3 — Cratehand Pressure
DC: 13
The Cratehand Guild arrives.
Not to collect.
To intimidate.
Key NPC: Branik Cratehand

## Phase 4 — Midnight Finale
DC: 16
## Scene
- Heavy rain
- Thick magical fog
- Midnight bells
- Overloaded Beverage Engine

---
## Fogcutter Crowd Event Table

Roll a **d12** whenever you want the market to feel alive.

| d12 | Event                                                                                                                                                    |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | The Fogcutter releases a blast of harmless glowing fog. Customers LOVE it. More costumers arrive this phase                                              |
| 2   | Steam erupts from the coffee pipes and covers the stall. Players must shout orders through the fog. DC 10 Instinct check                                 |
| 3   | A dockworker loudly declares the coffee “strong enough to revive corpses.” Sales immediately spike. Gain double money for the next 3 bevrages.           |
| 4   | Eleanor disappears underneath the stall yelling: “DON’T TOUCH THE BLUE VALVE.” Something immediately hisses. Costumers leave this phase                  |
| 5   | A customer accidentally drinks **The Tinker’s Last Cup** too quickly and starts speaking with extreme confidence. DC 15 Prescence check to calm him down |
| 6   | A nearby lantern explodes from excess Fogcutter energy. Nobody is hurt, but people panic briefly.                                                        |
| 7   | Someone in the crowd starts a chant supporting Eleanor. The whole market gets louder and more energetic.                                                 |
| 8   | The smell of Lampoil Lattes spreads through the district, drawing in curious townsfolk.                                                                  |
| 9   | A child steals coffee beans and the crowd turns it into a game trying to catch them.                                                                     |
| 10  | The Fogcutter briefly cuts through the mist, revealing the swamp beyond the walls. The crowd falls silent.                                               |
| 11  | Cratehand Guild members arrive and begin heckling customers. Players must react quickly.                                                                 |
| 12  | The machine begins to overload violently. Eleanor needs immediate help stabilizing it. Add 1 Instability.                                                |

---
## Final Challenge

Players must balance:

|Problem|Solution Examples|
|---|---|
|Panicking crowd|Presence|
|Breaking machinery|Knowledge|
|Rushing orders|Agility|
|Carrying supplies|Strength|
|Helping Eleanor emotionally|Instinct or Presence|

Run this as a fast-paced montage.
Each player acts once per round until:
- they reach 50 Handfulls,
- or the Guild takes the machine.
