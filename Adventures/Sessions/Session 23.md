## Last Session Recap: A Cycle of Death and Regret
Last session, the party came face-to-face with Queen Caelith Veyron and uncovered more of the terrible truth behind the Quell Trials. Through their conversations with the Queen and the Dollmaker, they began to understand the cruelty of the system that had sent countless adventurers toward their inevitable confrontation with Krallax. The promise of victory had long been used to fuel the trials, but the truth was far darker than they had imagined.

Their encounter with the Dollmaker revealed just how deeply he had been manipulated. His body had been mechanically altered, with a mysterious device embedded in his vertebrae, binding him to the horrors he had been forced to create. Behind his monstrous creations was a broken man, twisted by years of torment and control, whose own work had become a source of unimaginable regret.

Now, with the Dollmaker regaining consciousness, the party faces a new challenge. Rather than accepting the fate that has claimed so many adventurers before them, they set out to find the surviving groups who have been caught in the same vicious cycle. If they are to stand a chance against Krallax, they will need more than courage and determination. They will need allies, answers, and the strength to challenge the system that has kept this cycle alive.

The road ahead remains uncertain, but one thing has become clear: the party is no longer fighting simply to defeat a monster. They are fighting to end a cycle of death and regret, and to ensure that no one else is forced to suffer the same fate.
