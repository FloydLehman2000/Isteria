## Properties

>**Journal Fragment — Ethereal Voice**
I reached the hollow where the world forgets how to echo.
It is a place that does not welcome footsteps so much as absorb them. The air is thick with old pressure, as if the stone itself has been holding its breath for centuries and never learned how to release it. Nothing grows here. Nothing decays properly either. Even ruin feels reluctant.
The passage down was not a descent—it was a surrender to depth that had no bottom, only agreement.
At the heart of it all waited the sarcophagus.
Not ornate. Not sacred in any way that invites prayer. It was carved with the kind of precision that suggests repetition—something made again and again until the hands forgot why they were shaping it. It did not call attention to itself. It simply refused to be moved, as though existence had been negotiated around it rather than imposed upon it.
I brought **The Last Light**.
It felt heavier here, though nothing in the world could explain why. The weight was not in my hands, but in the act of carrying it this far. As if the journey itself had been the burden, and this moment only revealed it.
The sarcophagus did not open.
It accepted.
That is the only word that fits what happened—acceptance without ceremony, without resistance, without witness. The stone shifted not like mechanism, but like memory recalling where something belongs.
I placed **The Last Light** within.
For an instant, the hollow beneath the world felt less like absence and more like completion. Not peace—stone does not know peace—but alignment. As if something long displaced had finally been returned to the only place that could hold its contradiction.
When the lid settled back, there was no sound that deserved to be remembered.
Only the sense that whatever light had been carried this far would no longer be carried at all.
It would remain here, where even forgetting cannot reach cleanly.
