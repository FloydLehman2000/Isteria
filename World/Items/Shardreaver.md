## 🗡️ **Shardreaver – Soul-Forged Blade (Rare, Sentient Weapon)**

### Weapon Type:
- **Light Weapon** (can be dual-wielded)
- **One-Handed Sword or Dagger (Player’s Choice)**

---
### ✨ **Weapon Traits:**
- **Finesse, Light, Soul-Bound (Requires Attunement)**
- Can be used by all classes but grants unique benefits to Fighters and Rogues.

---

### 📜 **Weapon Properties:**
#### **1. Soul Drinker (Trait)**
When you strike an enemy with Shardreaver, you may **mark them with a Soul Fragment.**
- **Once per turn**, when you hit a marked enemy, you deal **+1 Dagger damage** per soul fragment (max +3).
- Killing a marked enemy causes their soul fragment to be absorbed by Shardreaver, healing you for **1 hit point**.

---
#### **2. Sentient Whisper (Narrative Trait)**
Shardreaver is alive with fragmented memories of past adventurers.
- The blade **whispers to you** (the GM provides cryptic hints, warnings, or misleading suggestions depending on Shardreaver’s mood).

---
### 🕯️ **Drawback: Soul Hunger**
If Shardreaver goes **three consecutive sessions** without harvesting at least one soul fragment, it begins to
- Weigh heavily in your hand (may impose a -1 to attacks until it feeds).

---
### 💀 **Deep Secret (GM Knowledge)**
Shardreaver previously contained the soul and consiousness of Vealen Duskthorn. Now the mind and soul of Crane has taken over and is here to stay.
