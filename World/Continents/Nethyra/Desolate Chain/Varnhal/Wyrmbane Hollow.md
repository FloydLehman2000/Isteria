## Crypt of Xeana, The Fading Light
Hidden deep within the mist-covered ruins of [[Wyrmbane Hollow]] lies the final resting place of **Xeana, The Fading Light**, one of the highest-ranking priests ever to serve [[Lady Luma]]. The crypt predates the Ascension and was constructed during an age when faith still shaped kingdoms and divine influence was considered a tangible force within the world. Unlike the grand temples that once honored Luma openly, Xeana's crypt was intentionally built to disappear. No roads lead to it. No surviving maps accurately mark its location. Even within the Hollow itself, travelers often wander past its entrance without realizing it exists.

The structure is remarkably simple for someone of Xeana's importance. Pale stone corridors descend beneath the ruins into a circular burial chamber illuminated by an impossible starlight that has endured for centuries without flame or fuel. The walls are covered with carefully preserved histories, prayers, and observations written by generations of Luma's faithful. Rather than glorifying gods or heroes, these records focus on remembrance itself. Entire lives have been preserved here. Forgotten names, vanished villages, and stories that would otherwise have disappeared from history remain etched into the stone. The crypt was never meant to celebrate divinity. It was built to preserve memory.

At the heart of the chamber rests Xeana's sarcophagus. It bears no statues, no treasures, and no declarations of holiness. Only a single phrase is repeated throughout the stonework:

> _What is remembered survives._

Buried alongside Xeana lies the relic known as **[[The Lantern of Stars]]**. For centuries, historians believed Xeana's purpose was simply to safeguard the artifact until it was needed once again. The truth, however, was deliberately erased from history. The Lantern was never hidden for protection. It was hidden for containment.

Long ago, the leaders of Luma's faith discovered that **The Lantern of Stars** and **The Last Light** were not separate relics at all. They were pieces of a greater whole, intentionally divided and entrusted to different guardians. The records explaining why were eventually destroyed, leaving only fragments and speculation behind. Xeana was among the last people entrusted with the complete truth.

When brought near one another, the two relics begin to reunite. No ritual is required. No command can stop it. They simply recognize what they once were and become whole again.

The consequence of this reunion is the return of **Asema Wyrm**.

Once, Asema served as one of Lady Luma's most devoted celestial attendants. She wandered the world preserving stories, guiding pilgrims, and ensuring knowledge endured through the passing of generations. Yet as faith slowly diminished and fewer mortals looked toward the heavens, Asema became convinced that remembrance alone was no longer enough. Where Lady Luma saw faith as something that should be freely chosen, Asema saw weakness. She came to believe that fear could accomplish what devotion could not.

Descending among those she called the _Groundfolk_, Asema began spreading terror in Luma's name. Villages burned beneath blue celestial fire. Pilgrims were driven toward fanaticism through visions and threats. Entire communities came to worship not out of reverence, but out of fear of being forgotten or punished. To Asema, these actions were necessary. If mortals feared the gods enough, they would never abandon them again.

Lady Luma disagreed.

The goddess had never desired worship born from fear. Her faith was built upon preservation, knowledge, and remembrance. Asema's actions corrupted those ideals entirely. Rather than protecting belief, she had weaponized it. Rather than preserving memory, she had transformed it into a tool of control.

In response, Asema was stripped of her purpose and sealed away. The relics were divided. The records were buried. And the responsibility of ensuring the truth remained forgotten fell to Xeana and those who followed her. For centuries, they succeeded.

Now, as explorers descend into Wyrmbane Hollow carrying **The Last Light**, that ancient act of forgetting begins to unravel. Should the relics reunite once more, Asema Wyrm will return—not as a monster, nor as a demon, but as exactly what she believes herself to be.

The savior of a faith that never asked to be saved.

## Cratehand Guild Outpost

At the entrance to [[Wyrmbane Hollow]], the [[Cratehands Guild]] has established what has become the largest expeditionary operation in the region. The approach to the Hollow is entirely dominated by a towering wooden barricade stretching from one rocky ridge to the other, sealing off the mist-covered ruins beyond. Reinforced timber walls, sharpened stakes, and elevated watch platforms transform the entrance into something closer to a military checkpoint than an archaeological dig site. A massive set of double doors serves as the only official route into the Hollow, guarded day and night by guild mercenaries tasked with monitoring both incoming adventurers and those fortunate enough to return.

Behind the wall lies a sprawling encampment built around a singular obsession: understanding what waits within Wyrmbane Hollow. What began as a small surveying operation has grown into a permanent settlement of cartographers, scholars, mercenaries, laborers, and relic hunters. Supply wagons arrive weekly. New tents appear every month. Yet despite the resources invested into the operation, progress remains painfully slow.

The heart of the camp belongs to [[Veyla Cratch]]. Her command tent stands at its center, surrounded by messengers and scouts carrying reports from the latest expeditions. Inside, tables overflow with maps, sketches, journals, excavation notes, and conflicting observations gathered from months of exploration. Entire sections of Wyrmbane Hollow seem incapable of remaining consistent between reports. Buildings appear in different locations. Streets terminate where they did not before. Structures thought cleared are later rediscovered by another team entirely. Veyla has become increasingly convinced that the Hollow cannot be understood through conventional mapping, yet continues the effort regardless. Every recovered fragment suggests something of immense importance lies buried somewhere beneath the ruins.

Surrounding the command tent are rows of soldier quarters housing the guards and mercenaries employed by the guild. Most are veterans of multiple expeditions into the Hollow, and few conversations last long before turning toward stories of what waits beyond the gate. Some speak of hearing voices in abandoned buildings. Others claim they encountered landmarks that vanished days later. Many possess maps of the Hollow. No two maps fully agree. Despite the uncertainty, the guild continues paying well enough that there is never a shortage of volunteers willing to venture inside.

The most crowded location in the camp is the medic tent. Wyrmbane Hollow has earned a reputation for allowing people to enter far more easily than it allows them to leave. Most scouting parties never return. Those that do are rarely unchanged. Missing limbs are common enough that extra prosthetics are kept on hand. Explorers frequently arrive suffering from exhaustion, strange memory lapses, or injuries they cannot explain. Some claim sections of the Hollow actively resist exploration. Others insist the ruins themselves are alive in some impossible way. The healers have long since stopped asking for coherent explanations. Experience has taught them that survivors rarely remember enough to provide one.

Months of exploration have at least revealed the nature of the Hollow itself. Beneath the mist lies an immense ruin predating the Ascension, perhaps even predating the oldest surviving historical records. Crumbling roads, collapsed towers, forgotten shrines, and entire districts of ancient architecture stretch throughout the valley. Many of these structures have become temporary waystations for exploration teams pushing deeper into the ruins. Others remain untouched, avoided not because they are known to be dangerous, but because nobody can remember what happened during previous attempts to investigate them.

Of all the expeditions sent into Wyrmbane Hollow, only one is believed to have successfully reached the crypt spoken of in local legends. The identities of those explorers remain unknown. No names accompany the account. No dates survive. Knowledge of their journey exists solely because of a fragment recovered by the Keepers of the Lost Tome from the [[Vault of Shar'Korath]].

The recovered document has become the foundation of every modern expedition into the Hollow.

The journal describes a descent through a place where memory itself seems uncertain. It speaks of a sarcophagus hidden beneath the world, of an impossible stillness that lingered around it, and of a relic known as **The Last Light** being returned to a resting place that seemed to recognize it. The final passages describe neither triumph nor discovery, but completion—as though something displaced had finally been returned to where it belonged.

For many within the Cratehands Guild, the journal is proof that the mythical tomb exists. For Veyla Cratch, it is proof that someone reached it first. And if someone reached it once, then someone can reach it again.

## Team Laze
After months of failed expeditions, missing scouts, and increasingly contradictory maps, only a handful of exploration teams continue venturing deep into [[Wyrmbane Hollow]]. Among them, none have traveled further than **Team Laze**.

Originally formed as a simple surveying crew contracted by the [[Cratehands Guild]], Team Laze has gradually become something of a legend within the outpost. They are one of the few groups that consistently return from the Hollow, and perhaps the only team that has mapped portions of the deeper ruins and lived long enough to discuss them. While many adventurers boast about what they have seen inside the mist, Team Laze rarely exaggerates their stories. The simple fact that they keep coming back has earned them a reputation few others possess.

When the party arrives, Team Laze is preparing for what may become their deepest expedition yet. Recent discoveries recovered from the Hollow suggest that several previously inaccessible sections of the ruins have become reachable. Combined with the information found within the [[Vault of Shar'Korath]], Veyla Cratch believes they may finally be approaching the location of the mythical crypt itself.

Normally, no outsiders would be permitted to join such an expedition. However, if the party earns enough trust or convinces [[Veyla Cratch]] that they are more asset than liability, she may allow them to accompany Team Laze into the Hollow. Most members of the guild consider this either a tremendous opportunity or a death sentence.
### Hawk Leader
The oldest member of Team Laze and its unquestioned commander is an elderly elf known almost exclusively by his nickname, **Hawk Leader**. His real name is **Aurelian Hawkstride**, though few people still use it.

Years of expeditions have left his body covered in scars, burns, and poorly healed injuries. One ear bears the marks of an old beast attack, and a jagged scar stretches across his jawline. Despite his age, Aurelian moves with surprising confidence and possesses an uncanny ability to navigate dangerous environments.

Nobody is entirely sure why he continues exploring the Hollow. Some believe he is chasing a final great discovery before retirement. Others suspect he simply doesn't know how to stop.

Among the camp's explorers, Hawk Leader is respected because he rarely loses people. Not because he keeps them safe, but because he knows exactly when to run.
### Limp Queen
The team's arcanist, cartographer, and resident pessimist is a human woman known throughout the camp as **Limp Queen**. Her actual name is **Selene Marris**, though even she occasionally forgets to introduce herself by it.

Several years ago, during an expedition into the Hollow, Selene lost her left leg below the knee under circumstances she refuses to discuss. Rather than replace it with a conventional prosthetic, she commissioned a reinforced arcane support pole forged from salvaged Hollow metal. The device functions as both walking aid and defensive tool, allowing her to anchor herself against impacts that would send others flying.

Selene specializes in identifying magical anomalies and deciphering ancient ruins. More importantly, she is one of the few people who meticulously compares expedition reports after every journey. Because of this, she was among the first to realize that portions of the Hollow appear inconsistent between visits.

She treats the Hollow less like a ruin and more like a problem attempting to disguise itself as a location.
### Grizzle
Unlike the others, **Grizzle** has never bothered using a nickname. His name is simply Grizzle.

The aging orc serves as the team's front-line muscle and has survived enough disastrous expeditions that many newcomers assume he must be impossible to kill. His left eye was lost during an expedition several years ago, though the story changes depending on how much he's had to drink. Some versions involve monsters. Others involve gambling. One version involves a goat. Only Grizzle knows the truth.

Despite his age, he remains an intimidating presence. Massive even by orcish standards, he fights wearing reinforced gauntlets fitted with crackling arcane conductors. Every punch lands with a burst of sparks and thunderous force capable of shattering stone. While Hawk Leader provides caution and Selene provides intelligence, Grizzle provides certainty. If something needs breaking, moving, punching, or intimidating, he is usually the solution.

He also appears to genuinely enjoy nearly everything about exploring Wyrmbane Hollow. This concerns the other two greatly.
