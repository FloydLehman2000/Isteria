## Lore
---
Fallen sanctum of Shar'Korath is a ruin guarded by the [[Cratehands Guild]] having information about many a things. But the most important thing being information about [[Ithar Daughter of Luma]]. This fallen sanctum is a library filled with information. Most of it is rotten and fallen apart. But the Vault of Shar'Korath contains a book called: [[Keeper of the Lost]]. Containing information about Lady Luma and her fallen children. The information at value is the possible location of [[The Lantern of Stars]]. Which coincidently lies in the area named [[Wyrmbane Hollow]]. Now this area is guarded heavily by the [[Cratehands Guild]] since they think based on their information this can be a powerful relic that will help them take the winning hand in the [[Trade War]].

## Layout
---
The Fallen sanctum of Shar'Korath is build like a small village with a couple of houses at the northern point of Varnhall. Close to the edge they surrounded a single tower that they considered to be a place of knowledge about history and more.

## Sanctum of Shar'Korath
A stone building stands in the middle of this small village. Stone crooked, moss growing on the sides, surrounded by muddy water and fallen trees. It has a old look to it as the top of it is half broken off laying on the northern side of the tower. A few windows surround this building, noting 2 floors. the third floor broken and turned to rubble. Now being guarded by henchmen of the [[Cratehands Guild]]. If the party enters with no care the guards will ambush them.

### Entrance (Fist floor)
The entrance hall of Shar’Korath feels less like a building and more like something that was _left behind_. The stone floor is cracked in wide branching patterns, as though the structure itself tried to escape the earth. Dust hangs thick in the air, disturbed only by the recent movement of the [[Cratehands Guild]] guards.

Broken lecterns and collapsed shelving line the walls, suggesting this once served as a public archive or reading hall. Most texts have rotted into unreadable pulp, but fragments of arcane preservation still flicker faintly in the corners of the room.

If the party enters quietly, they can avoid immediate detection. If not, the guards stationed here will attempt to isolate and ambush intruders before they can reach the deeper sanctum.
#### Points of Interest
- A shattered mural depicting a robed figure holding a fractured Moon — likely symbolic of Lady Luma and her descendants
- A collapsed staircase leading upward, partially blocked by rubble (dangerous climb or forced clearing required)
- A faint magical hum coming from beneath the floorboards, suggesting something sealed below
#### Encounter: Cratehands Wardens
A small group of guild enforcers patrols the entrance.
They are not ordinary looters — they move with purpose, as if they already know what they are searching for.

### Second Floor (Collapsed Archive)
The second floor is partially intact, though dangerously unstable. Entire sections of the ceiling have caved inward, forming natural choke points and vertical gaps between rooms. The architecture suggests this was once the heart of the sanctum’s knowledge storage. Now it is a graveyard of knowledge. Books, scrolls, and carved stone tablets are scattered across the floor, most of them irreparably damaged. However, a few preserved sections remain intact due to faint protective enchantments that still cling to the stone.
#### Points of Interest
- A reading chamber with intact shelving containing fragmented records of Shar’Korath’s founding
- A collapsed study desk with a locked iron compartment (requires DC 12 Strength or Finnesse to open, contains 2 HF)
- A circular observation window overlooking Varnhall, now cracked and partially boarded
#### Discoverable Lore
- A repeated warning phrase etched into multiple surfaces:  
    _“Knowledge is not meant to remain still.”_

### Vault of Shar’Korath (Sealed Chamber)
Deep within the sanctum lies the only section that still feels _intact_. Unlike the rest of the ruin, this chamber has resisted decay almost entirely. The stone is smooth, unnaturally preserved, and covered in layered arcane seals that pulse faintly when approached. The Vault does not feel abandoned. It feels _guarded_. The air inside is colder, heavier — as though the concept of knowledge itself has been compressed into this space. At the center stands a single stone pedestal.

Upon it rests the preserved text known as:
**[[Keeper of the Lost]]**
The book is bound in an unknown material that resists time, damage, and decay. Its pages are not fully visible until touched, as though the information itself reacts to intent rather than sight.
